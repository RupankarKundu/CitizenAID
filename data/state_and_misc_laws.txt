=== BANKING AND FINANCIAL GRIEVANCES ===

RBI OMBUDSMAN SCHEME (Integrated Ombudsman 2021):
Covers complaints against: Scheduled Commercial Banks, NBFCs, Payment System Participants, Credit Information Companies.
Process:
1. First complain to bank's own grievance redressal. Wait 30 days.
2. If no satisfactory response within 30 days, file complaint at cms.rbi.org.in
3. RBI Ombudsman is FREE — no fees.
4. Covers: unauthorized transactions, failed ATM transactions, debit without credit, inoperative accounts, mis-selling of insurance/mutual funds, excessive charges, non-implementation of court orders, illegal recovery agents.
5. Award can be up to Rs. 20 lakhs. For consequential losses, up to Rs. 1 crore.
Helpline: 14448

INSURANCE GRIEVANCES:
1. First complain to Grievance Redressal Officer of insurance company. Wait 15 days.
2. If unsatisfied, approach Insurance Ombudsman (free) within 1 year. Find your Ombudsman at cioins.co.in
3. Can also approach IRDAI at complaints.irdai.gov.in or call 155255.
4. Claim cannot be rejected for technical defect if premium was accepted.
5. Repudiation of claim must be explained in writing.

SEBI INVESTOR COMPLAINTS:
File complaints against brokers/companies at scores.sebi.gov.in
SCORES (SEBI Complaints Redress System) — free.
Also approach stock exchange grievance cells and investor service centres.

=== TELECOM GRIEVANCES ===

TRAI (Telecom Regulatory Authority of India):
1. First complain to your telecom operator's customer care. Get reference number.
2. If not resolved in 30 days, escalate to Nodal Officer of the telecom company.
3. If still not resolved in 30 days, file complaint with Appellate Authority.
4. For regulatory issues, complaint to TRAI at trai.gov.in
5. Common issues covered: call drop, billing disputes, unsolicited commercial calls (DND), poor service quality, porting issues.

DND (Do Not Disturb) Registry:
Register your number on DND by SMS "START DND" to 1909. Complaints about promotional calls after DND registration can be filed at 1909 or trai.gov.in/consumers

=== REAL ESTATE (RERA) ===

RERA (Real Estate Regulation Act 2016):
All residential/commercial projects above 500 sq meters or 8 apartments must be registered with State RERA.
Rights of Buyers:
1. Full information about project including sanctioned plans, approvals, completion date
2. Compensation for delay in possession
3. Refund with interest if builder fails to complete project
4. Structural defect rectification for 5 years after possession

How to file RERA complaint:
1. Check if your project is registered at your state RERA website (e.g., maharera.mahaonline.gov.in for Maharashtra)
2. File online complaint with documentary evidence
3. Adjudicating Officer can award compensation
4. No court fees — only nominal RERA filing fee

Common RERA reliefs:
- Delayed possession: Interest @SBI MCLR + 2% on deposited amount for delay period
- Defective title: Full refund + interest
- Structural defects: Free rectification within 30 days

=== FOOD AND DRUG SAFETY ===

FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India):
File complaints about adulterated food, mislabeling, unsanitary conditions at food.safety@fssai.gov.in or call 1800-11-2100
State Food Safety Officer handles local complaints.

DRUG PRICE COMPLAINTS:
If a medical shop charges more than MRP: File complaint with Drug Control Authority of your state.
Essential medicines price list available at NPPA website. If charged above NPPA notified price, file complaint at NPPA (nppa.gov.in).

=== ENVIRONMENTAL RIGHTS ===

Right to clean environment is part of Article 21 (Right to Life).
Industrial pollution complaints: State Pollution Control Board (SPCB)
National Green Tribunal (NGT): Can approach NGT for environmental violations. NGT has powers of a civil court and can award compensation and give directions. File at ngtonline.nic.in

=== LAND ACQUISITION ===

Right to Fair Compensation and Transparency in Land Acquisition Act, 2013:
- Government must give Social Impact Assessment before acquiring land
- Minimum compensation: 2x market value in urban areas, 3x-4x in rural areas + additional
- Land acquired for private companies requires consent of 80% landowners
- Land acquired for PPP requires consent of 70% landowners
- If land not used within 5 years, it must be returned

=== POLICE COMPLAINTS AUTHORITY ===

Supreme Court in Prakash Singh case (2006) mandated:
- Police Complaints Authority at State level and District level
- Separate from police administration
- Handles: serious misconduct, custodial violence, illegal arrest, illegal detention
- State Lokayukta also handles police complaints in many states

NHRC (National Human Rights Commission):
Complaints about human rights violations by government officials including police.
File at nhrc.nic.in or call 1800-11-8188

STATE INFORMATION COMMISSIONS:
After exhausting First Appeal, approach State Information Commission for state government RTI matters.
For Central Government RTI, approach Central Information Commission (CIC) at cic.gov.in

=== DISABILITY RIGHTS ===

Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016:
- 5% reservation in government jobs
- Free education for disabled children
- Barrier-free access to public buildings
- Reservation in poverty alleviation schemes
- Insurance schemes must not discriminate
- Chief Commissioner for Persons with Disabilities handles complaints

=== SC/ST RIGHTS (Special Reference) ===

Schedule Caste/Schedule Tribe (Prevention of Atrocities) Act 1989 & 2015 Amendment:
Protects against: humiliation, forcible tonsure, removal of clothes, public insult, physical violence, land occupation, interference with right to vote, false cases.
- Immediate FIR must be registered — no preliminary inquiry
- Free legal aid to victims
- Special courts for speedy trial
- Victim compensation scheme
- Appeal directly to High Court if police refuses to register FIR
