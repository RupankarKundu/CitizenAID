CYBER FINANCIAL FRAUD — ACT IMMEDIATELY
If you have lost money in online fraud, every minute matters:
STEP 1: Call 1930 (Cyber Financial Crime Helpline) IMMEDIATELY
STEP 2: Report at cybercrime.gov.in
STEP 3: Visit nearest police station with Cyber Crime cell
The faster you report, the higher the chance of freezing the fraudster's account and recovering money.

TYPES OF ONLINE FRAUD AND HOW TO HANDLE EACH

OTP FRAUD / SIM SWAP
Fraudster calls pretending to be bank/telecom, tricks you into sharing OTP:
- Never share OTP with anyone — banks never ask for OTP
- Immediately call your bank's helpline and block the account
- File complaint at 1930 and cybercrime.gov.in
- File FIR at police — Section 66C IT Act (identity theft) + Section 318 BNS (cheating)

UPI FRAUD / PAYMENT APP SCAMS
Fraudster sends collect request or tricks you into paying:
- File complaint on NPCI portal and your payment app
- Call 1930 immediately
- File cybercrime complaint online

FAKE JOB OFFERS / WORK FROM HOME SCAMS
Promised high-paying jobs, asked to pay registration/training fee:
- File FIR at cyber cell — Section 318 BNS (cheating) + IT Act
- Report to Ministry of Labour job fraud portal
- Report to National Consumer Helpline 1800-11-4000

LOAN APP HARASSMENT / ILLEGAL LENDING APPS
Illegal lending apps charge extreme interest, harass and threaten contacts:
- Do NOT pay more than the principal borrowed
- File complaint at RBI sachet.rbi.org.in
- File cybercrime complaint at cybercrime.gov.in
- File FIR at police for criminal intimidation — Section 351 BNS
- RBI has banned many such apps — report to RBI immediately

ONLINE SHOPPING FRAUD (FAKE WEBSITES/SELLERS)
Paid for product but not delivered, fake product received:
- File complaint at consumer helpline: consumerhelpline.gov.in
- File cybercrime complaint if significant amount
- Dispute the transaction with your bank (chargeback)
- File FIR — Section 318 BNS (cheating) + IT Act Section 66D

SEXTORTION / BLACKMAIL WITH INTIMATE IMAGES
Someone is blackmailing you with private photos or videos:
- Do NOT pay — paying encourages more demands
- Immediately file cybercrime complaint at cybercrime.gov.in
- File FIR at Women's cell or cyber cell
- Offences: Section 67A IT Act (publishing obscene material) — 7 years imprisonment, Section 354C IPC/BNS (voyeurism), Section 508 IPC (blackmail)
- Report content to the platform (Instagram, WhatsApp, etc.) for removal
- National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal has specific section for this

SOCIAL MEDIA ACCOUNT HACKING
Someone has hacked your Facebook/Instagram/WhatsApp account:
- Immediately try account recovery through official platform process
- Report to platform: Facebook, Instagram, Google — all have hacked account recovery
- File cybercrime complaint — Section 66 IT Act (unauthorized access) — 3 years + fine
- If account used to cheat others: file FIR immediately

FAKE PROFILES AND IDENTITY THEFT
Someone created fake profile in your name:
- Report profile to the platform immediately
- File cybercrime complaint — Section 66C IT Act (identity theft) — 3 years + Rs. 1 lakh fine
- File FIR at cyber cell

CYBER BULLYING AND ONLINE HARASSMENT
Repeatedly receiving offensive messages, trolling, threats:
- Block and report the user on the platform
- Take screenshots as evidence
- File cybercrime complaint
- If threats to life: file FIR immediately under Section 351/352 BNS (criminal intimidation)
- Women can specifically use SHe-Box portal: shebox.nic.in

PHISHING EMAILS / FAKE BANK WEBSITES
Received email asking to update bank details or click suspicious link:
- Never click suspicious links
- Forward phishing email to your bank's fraud email
- Report to cybercrime.gov.in
- If you clicked and shared info: immediately call bank helpline to block account

INVESTMENT FRAUD / PONZI SCHEMES / CRYPTO SCAMS
Promised guaranteed high returns, money taken and disappeared:
- File FIR at economic offences wing of police
- Report to SEBI at scores.sebi.gov.in if securities involved
- Report to RBI if fake bank/NBFC
- File cybercrime complaint
- These are typically Section 318 BNS (cheating) + Section 420 IPC cases

HOW TO FILE CYBERCRIME COMPLAINT ONLINE
Step 1: Go to cybercrime.gov.in
Step 2: Click "Report Cyber Crime"
Step 3: Select category of crime
Step 4: Fill in details — provide screenshots, transaction IDs, phone numbers, URLs
Step 5: You will receive acknowledgement number
Step 6: Police will contact you for further investigation

EVIDENCE TO PRESERVE
Always preserve before reporting:
- Screenshots of all conversations, transactions, profiles
- Transaction IDs, UTR numbers, account numbers
- Phone numbers, email addresses of fraudster
- URLs of fake websites
- Recording of fraudulent calls if possible

INTERMEDIARY LIABILITY
Under IT Act, platforms (Facebook, WhatsApp, Google) must:
- Remove illegal content within 36 hours of receiving complaint
- Remove content related to sexual crimes within 24 hours
- Maintain records of users
- Cooperate with government investigations
- File complaint with Ministry of Electronics if platform does not act

DATA PRIVACY RIGHTS
- No organization should share your personal data without consent
- Banks, hospitals, companies cannot sell your data
- Aadhaar data misuse: file complaint with UIDAI
- Credit score misuse: file complaint with CIBIL/Equifax and RBI
- Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 provides rights to Indian citizens
