HOUSING SCHEMES

PRADHAN MANTRI AWAS YOJANA — URBAN (PMAY-U)
Eligibility: Annual income up to Rs. 18 lakhs (different slabs).
- EWS (Economically Weaker Section): income up to Rs. 3 lakhs — subsidy up to Rs. 2.67 lakhs
- LIG (Low Income Group): income Rs. 3-6 lakhs — subsidy up to Rs. 2.35 lakhs
- MIG I: income Rs. 6-12 lakhs — subsidy up to Rs. 2.30 lakhs
- MIG II: income Rs. 12-18 lakhs — subsidy up to Rs. 2.30 lakhs
How to apply: Through banks and housing finance companies. Track at pmaymis.gov.in.
If application rejected or delayed: file RTI with Urban Local Body asking for reason.

PRADHAN MANTRI AWAS YOJANA — GRAMIN (PMAY-G)
For rural BPL families to build pucca house. Financial assistance: Rs. 1.20 lakhs (plain areas), Rs. 1.30 lakhs (hilly/difficult areas). Applied through Gram Panchayat. Track at rhreporting.nic.in. If your name is not in list despite eligibility: file complaint with Block Development Officer and file RTI.

FOOD SECURITY

PM GARIB KALYAN ANNA YOJANA (PMGKAY)
Free food grains (5 kg per person/month) to National Food Security Act beneficiaries. Eligible: ration card holders under Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) and Priority Household (PHH). Collect from Fair Price Shop (ration shop) monthly.

NATIONAL FOOD SECURITY ACT (NFSA) RATION
- AAY (yellow card): 35 kg per household/month at Rs. 1-3 per kg
- PHH (pink/other cards): 5 kg per person/month at Rs. 1-3 per kg
- If denied ration: file complaint with District Supply Officer or State Food Commission
- Online grievance: most states have food department portals

HOW TO GET RATION CARD
Apply at ration office or online at state food department portal. Documents needed: Aadhaar, address proof, income certificate, family members' Aadhaar cards. If wrongfully rejected: appeal to District Supply Officer, then State Food Commission. File RTI asking for eligibility criteria and reason for rejection.

EMPLOYMENT AND INCOME

MGNREGA (MAHATMA GANDHI NATIONAL RURAL EMPLOYMENT GUARANTEE ACT)
- Guarantees 100 days of paid work per year to every rural household
- Current wage: Rs. 220-300+ per day (varies by state)
- Work must be provided within 15 days of application, within 5 km of home
- If work not provided: unemployment allowance must be paid (25% of wage for first 30 days, 50% thereafter)
- Wages must be paid within 15 days of work completion
- How to apply: register at Gram Panchayat. Get job card.
- Check your MGNREGA account at nrega.nic.in
- If wages delayed or denied: file complaint at nrega.nic.in or with District Programme Coordinator

PM SVANidhi (STREET VENDOR SCHEME)
Working capital loans for street vendors:
- First loan: Rs. 10,000 — repay in 1 year
- Second loan: Rs. 20,000
- Third loan: Rs. 50,000
- Interest subsidy provided
- Apply at nearby bank or PM SVANidhi portal: pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in
- Need certificate from Urban Local Body as street vendor

MUDRA LOAN (PM MUDRA YOJANA)
Loans for small businesses without collateral:
- Shishu: up to Rs. 50,000 (new businesses)
- Kishore: Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 5 lakhs (growing businesses)
- Tarun: Rs. 5 lakhs to Rs. 10 lakhs (established businesses)
- Apply at any bank or NBFC
- No processing fee for Shishu loans
- For women entrepreneurs: additional benefits

HEALTHCARE SCHEMES

AYUSHMAN BHARAT PM-JAY
- Health insurance of Rs. 5 lakhs per family per year
- Covers hospitalization, surgery, medicines, diagnostics
- No premium to be paid by beneficiary
- Valid at empanelled government and private hospitals
- Check eligibility: pmjay.gov.in or call 14555 (toll-free)
- Get Ayushman card from Common Service Centre or hospital
- Documents: ration card or other government ID

PM JAN AROGYA YOJANA STATE EXTENSIONS
Many states have extended PM-JAY with additional benefits:
- West Bengal: Swasthya Sathi scheme — Rs. 5 lakhs coverage for all families
- Tamil Nadu: Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance
- Karnataka: Arogya Karnataka
- Check your state health department for state-specific schemes

JANANI SURAKSHA YOJANA
Cash incentive for institutional delivery:
- Rural areas: Rs. 1,400 for mother + Rs. 600 for ASHA worker
- Urban areas: Rs. 1,000 for mother + Rs. 400 for ASHA
- Applicable for mothers below poverty line
- Apply at government hospital during delivery

FREE MEDICINES SCHEME
Many state governments provide free medicines at government hospitals:
- Rajasthan: Mukhyamantri Nishulk Dava Yojana
- Tamil Nadu: Free medicines in government hospitals
- Delhi: Free medicines and diagnostics
- Ask at your district hospital — you are entitled to prescribed medicines free

WOMEN AND GIRL CHILD SCHEMES

BETI BACHAO BETI PADHAO
Various benefits for girl child education and welfare. Implemented through health, education and women development departments.

PM MATRU VANDANA YOJANA (PMMVY)
Financial assistance for first pregnancy: Rs. 5,000 in 3 instalments. Condition: early registration, at least 2 antenatal checkups, institutional delivery. Apply at Anganwadi centre or health facility.

SUKANYA SAMRIDDHI YOJANA
Savings scheme for girl child (below 10 years): 8.2% interest. Tax-free returns. Minimum deposit Rs. 250/year, maximum Rs. 1.5 lakhs/year. Open at post office or bank. Matures when girl turns 21.

FARMER SCHEMES

PM KISAN SAMMAN NIDHI
Rs. 6,000 per year (Rs. 2,000 every 4 months) directly to farmer's bank account. For all landholding farmers. Check status at pmkisan.gov.in. If eligible but not receiving: file grievance at pmkisan.gov.in or contact local agriculture office.

PRADHAN MANTRI FASAL BIMA YOJANA
Crop insurance scheme for farmers. Covers crop loss due to natural calamities, pests, diseases. Premium: 2% for Kharif crops, 1.5% for Rabi crops, 5% for commercial crops. Balance premium paid by government. Apply at bank where agricultural loan is taken or at nearest bank.

HOW TO CHECK GOVERNMENT SCHEME STATUS USING RTI
If you applied for any scheme and have not received benefit:
1. Wait for prescribed time period
2. Enquire at implementing office
3. File RTI asking: application status, reason for delay/rejection, list of beneficiaries selected, criteria used for selection
4. Escalate to higher authority if no response
5. File complaint at CM Helpline or CPGRAMS: pgportal.gov.in

GRIEVANCE PORTALS FOR GOVERNMENT SCHEMES
- Central: pgportal.gov.in (CPGRAMS)
- PM Kisan: pmkisan.gov.in
- MGNREGA: nrega.nic.in
- Ration/PDS: Respective state food department portal
- PM Awas: pmaymis.gov.in (urban), rhreporting.nic.in (rural)
- PM-JAY: pmjay.gov.in or 14555
- Helpline for all central schemes: 1800-11-0001 (DARPG)
