SOURCE: Motor Vehicles Act 1988 (amended 2019) & Motor Accident Claims Tribunal | CATEGORY: Motor Vehicles & Accidents | JURISDICTION: India

OVERVIEW
The Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act 2019 significantly increased penalties and compensation. Every road accident victim has the right to compensation regardless of fault.

WHAT TO DO IMMEDIATELY AFTER AN ACCIDENT
Step 1: Call 112 (emergency) for ambulance and police
Step 2: Note down the vehicle number, driver details, insurance details of the other vehicle
Step 3: Take photos of the accident scene, damage, and injuries
Step 4: Get names and contact details of witnesses
Step 5: File FIR at the nearest police station — this is essential for insurance claims
Step 6: Get medical treatment — costs are claimable
Step 7: Inform your own insurance company within 24-48 hours

HIT AND RUN ACCIDENTS
If the vehicle that caused the accident fled:
- Compensation available from Solatium Fund (government fund for hit and run victims)
- Death: Rs. 2 lakhs compensation
- Grievous injury: Rs. 50,000 compensation
- Apply to Claims Tribunal or insurance company

THIRD PARTY INSURANCE — MANDATORY
Every vehicle must have at least Third Party (TP) insurance by law. TP insurance covers:
- Death or bodily injury to third parties (unlimited liability)
- Property damage up to Rs. 7.5 lakhs
- If the vehicle that hit you has TP insurance, you can claim from their insurer directly

HOW TO FILE MOTOR ACCIDENT CLAIM
Step 1: File FIR at police station (essential)
Step 2: Collect documents: FIR copy, medical bills, doctor's certificate, proof of income, vehicle RC and insurance details of other vehicle
Step 3: File claim petition before Motor Accident Claims Tribunal (MACT) at district level
Step 4: No court fee for filing MACT petition
Step 5: Tribunal will assess compensation and issue award
Step 6: Appeal to High Court if dissatisfied with award

COMPENSATION CALCULATION
Compensation depends on:
1. Medical expenses (actual bills)
2. Loss of income during treatment
3. Permanent disability (if any) — calculated as: monthly income × multiplier based on age
4. Pain and suffering
5. Funeral expenses (in case of death)
6. Loss of dependency (if breadwinner died) — calculated as: annual income × multiplier

For death: Compensation = Annual income × multiplier (based on age) + Rs. 15,000 funeral expenses + Rs. 40,000 loss of estate

CASHLESS TREATMENT — GOLDEN HOUR
Under MV Amendment 2019:
- Accident victims must receive free treatment in any hospital for first 48 hours (Golden Hour)
- Hospital cannot refuse treatment for want of money or police formalities
- Treatment cost borne by government and recovered later from insurance
- If hospital refuses: file complaint with State Medical Council immediately

INSURANCE CLAIM PROCESS
For own vehicle damage:
1. Intimate insurer within 24-48 hours
2. Don't move vehicle until surveyor inspects (or take photos first)
3. Take vehicle to authorized garage
4. Insurer will send surveyor to assess damage
5. Cashless claim: garage settles directly with insurer
6. Reimbursement claim: pay first, claim later

If insurer rejects or delays claim:
1. File grievance with insurer's Grievance Officer
2. Approach Insurance Ombudsman (free) within 1 year
3. File complaint with IRDAI at igms.irda.gov.in or call 155255
4. Approach Consumer Forum

TRAFFIC VIOLATIONS AND CHALLANS — REVISED PENALTIES (2019)
- Drunk driving: Rs. 10,000 first time, Rs. 15,000 second time + imprisonment
- Overspeeding: Rs. 1,000-2,000
- Without helmet: Rs. 1,000 + 3 months licence suspension
- Without seatbelt: Rs. 1,000
- Using mobile while driving: Rs. 5,000
- Without driving licence: Rs. 5,000
- Without vehicle insurance: Rs. 2,000 first time, Rs. 4,000 second time

HOW TO PAY E-CHALLAN
- Online at echallan.parivahan.gov.in
- Enter vehicle number or challan number
- Pay via UPI/card
- If you feel challan is wrong: contest at court mentioned on challan

DRIVING LICENCE — RIGHTS AND RULES
- DL cannot be seized without proper receipt
- If police seizes DL: demand Form 38A receipt
- Lost DL: apply at RTO for duplicate
- DL renewal: can be done online at parivahan.gov.in
- If DL suspended: you can appeal to Transport Authority

VEHICLE DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
- Registration Certificate (RC)
- Insurance Certificate (valid)
- Pollution Under Control Certificate (PUC)
- Driving Licence
- If commercial vehicle: Fitness Certificate, Permit

BUYING USED VEHICLE — PRECAUTIONS
- Check RC for hypothecation (loan pending)
- Check for blacklisting on Vahan portal: vahan.parivahan.gov.in
- Transfer RC within 30 days of purchase
- Transfer insurance in your name
- Check for pending challans
