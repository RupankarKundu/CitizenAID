INCOME TAX — BASICS FOR CITIZENS

WHO MUST FILE ITR
You must file Income Tax Return (ITR) if:
- Total income exceeds Rs. 2.5 lakhs per year (Rs. 3 lakhs for senior citizens 60+, Rs. 5 lakhs for 80+)
- You have foreign assets or income
- TDS has been deducted and you want refund
- You have deposited over Rs. 1 crore in bank accounts in a year
- You have paid electricity bills over Rs. 1 lakh
- You want to carry forward losses
- You have income from business or profession above threshold

TAX SLABS (NEW REGIME 2024-25)
- Up to Rs. 3 lakhs: No tax
- Rs. 3-6 lakhs: 5%
- Rs. 6-9 lakhs: 10%
- Rs. 9-12 lakhs: 15%
- Rs. 12-15 lakhs: 20%
- Above Rs. 15 lakhs: 30%
Note: Income up to Rs. 7 lakhs effectively tax-free due to rebate under Section 87A

HOW TO FILE ITR (ONLINE — FREE)
Step 1: Go to incometax.gov.in
Step 2: Register with PAN number
Step 3: Download Form 26AS and AIS (Annual Information Statement) — shows all TDS deducted
Step 4: Select correct ITR form (ITR-1 for salaried, ITR-4 for business)
Step 5: Fill in income details, deductions, tax paid
Step 6: Verify using Aadhaar OTP or net banking
Deadline: July 31 for individuals (without audit)

TDS (TAX DEDUCTED AT SOURCE)
Employer deducts TDS from salary. Bank deducts TDS on FD interest if interest exceeds Rs. 40,000/year (Rs. 50,000 for senior citizens). If your total income is below taxable limit: submit Form 15G (below 60 years) or Form 15H (senior citizens) to bank to prevent TDS deduction.

TAX REFUND — HOW TO GET
If more tax was deducted than your actual tax liability: file ITR to claim refund. Refund is credited directly to your bank account after processing. Track refund at incometax.gov.in. If refund delayed beyond 1 year: file grievance on e-filing portal.

INCOME TAX NOTICE — WHAT TO DO
If you receive an income tax notice:
- Read carefully — understand what is being asked
- Reply within the deadline mentioned
- Most notices are routine verification — don't panic
- Common notices: Section 139(9) — defective return, Section 143(1) — intimation, Section 148 — income escaped assessment
- If notice is for scrutiny: gather all documents and respond with evidence
- Free help available at Income Tax Seva Kendra at your local tax office
- Income Tax helpline: 1800-180-1961 (toll-free)

FORM 26AS — IMPORTANT DOCUMENT
Form 26AS shows all tax deducted/paid against your PAN. Download from incometax.gov.in or TRACES portal. If your employer or bank has deducted TDS but not deposited with government: the amount won't show in 26AS. File complaint with your Assessing Officer.

BANKING RIGHTS

BANK ACCOUNT — YOUR RIGHTS
- Every citizen has the right to open a bank account (Jan Dhan Yojana — zero balance account)
- Bank cannot refuse to open Basic Savings Account
- Minimum balance rules: must be communicated clearly; penalties cannot exceed Rs. 750/month for metro, less for rural
- Bank cannot charge for NEFT/RTGS/IMPS transactions done online

UNAUTHORIZED TRANSACTIONS — WHAT TO DO
If you see unauthorized debit in your account:
Step 1: Inform your bank WITHIN 3 DAYS by written complaint (email/letter)
Step 2: Block your card immediately through app or helpline
Step 3: File cybercrime complaint at cybercrime.gov.in and call 1930
If informed within 3 days: bank must reverse the amount. Delay in informing: your liability increases.

BANKING GRIEVANCES
Step 1: Contact branch manager in writing. Keep copy.
Step 2: Contact bank's Nodal Officer/Grievance Officer
Step 3: If not resolved in 30 days: file with RBI Ombudsman at cms.rbi.org.in (FREE)
Step 4: RBI Ombudsman can award up to Rs. 20 lakhs compensation
RBI helpline: 14448

LOAN AND EMI RIGHTS
- Banks must give you a clear loan agreement with all terms
- Prepayment penalty: not allowed on floating rate home loans
- EMI restructuring: you can request bank during financial hardship
- If you miss EMIs: bank must follow RBI NPA guidelines before declaring default
- Loan recovery agents cannot: use abusive language, threaten physically, visit at odd hours, contact third parties without your consent
- If recovery agents harass: file police complaint + complaint to RBI Ombudsman

CREDIT SCORE AND CIBIL
- Check your free credit report once a year at cibil.com, experian.in, crif.in, or equifax.in
- If credit score is wrong: file dispute with CIBIL within 30 days
- CIBIL must resolve dispute and correct errors
- File complaint with RBI if CIBIL does not correct genuine errors

INSURANCE — RIGHTS OF POLICYHOLDERS
- Free Look Period: 15-30 days after policy purchase to return it for full refund
- Insurance company must settle claim within 30 days of submission of complete documents
- If claim rejected: must give written reason
- If rejected without valid reason: approach Insurance Ombudsman at cioins.co.in (FREE)
- IRDAI Bima Bharosa: complaints at bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in or call 155255

MUTUAL FUNDS AND STOCK MARKET RIGHTS
- SEBI regulates all investment products
- Any investment advice: verify SEBI registration of advisor at sebi.gov.in
- Dispute with broker: approach exchange's Investor Grievance Cell, then SEBI SCORES portal
- Ponzi scheme or fraud: report to SEBI immediately at scores.sebi.gov.in
- Check broker registration: sebi.gov.in/sebiweb/home/HomeAction.do

PENSION — EPF AND NPS
EPF (Employees' Provident Fund):
- Check EPF balance: epfindia.gov.in or Umang app using UAN number
- Withdraw EPF online for unemployment (after 2 months), retirement, or medical emergency
- File grievance for EPF issues: epfigms.gov.in or call 1800-118-005
- Transfer EPF when changing jobs: use online transfer facility with UAN

NPS (National Pension System):
- Check NPS balance: cra-nsdl.com
- Partial withdrawal allowed for specific purposes after 3 years
- At 60: minimum 40% must be used to buy annuity, rest can be withdrawn

GST CONSUMER RIGHTS
- Every purchase above Rs. 200 in a shop: you have right to GST invoice
- Seller cannot refuse GST bill — it is your right and their obligation
- Check if business is GST registered: gst.gov.in (taxpayer search)
- Report GST fraud: gst.gov.in or call 1800-103-4786
