To,
The Public Information Officer,
[Name of Government Department/Ministry]
[Address]

Subject: Application under the Right to Information Act, 2005

Sir/Madam,

I, [Your Full Name], son/daughter of [Father's Name], residing at [Your Full Address], a citizen of India, wish to obtain the following information under Section 6 of the Right to Information Act, 2005:

1. [State your first query clearly and specifically]
2. [State your second query]
3. [State your third query]

[Period to which information relates: From _____ to _____]

I am enclosing/paying the application fee of Rs. 10/- by [Postal Order No. _____ / Cash Receipt / Demand Draft].

I declare that I am a citizen of India and the information sought is not covered under the exemptions listed in Section 8 of the RTI Act, 2005.

Please provide the information within 30 days as required under Section 7(1) of the RTI Act, 2005.

Thanking you,

Yours faithfully,
[Your Full Name]
[Address]
[Phone Number]
[Date]
[Signature]

=== RTI TEMPLATE FOR GOVERNMENT SCHEME STATUS ===

Subject: Seeking information regarding status of [scheme name] application/benefit

Queries:
1. What is the current status of my application for [scheme name] submitted on [date] with application number [number]?
2. Kindly provide the reason for delay/rejection of my application, if any.
3. Kindly provide the list of documents required for [scheme name] and confirm which documents are on record in my file.
4. Who is the responsible officer for processing [scheme name] applications in [block/district]?
5. What is the timeline prescribed for processing and disbursing benefits under [scheme name]?

---

=== RTI TEMPLATE FOR ROAD/INFRASTRUCTURE WORK ===

Subject: Seeking information regarding [road/building/infrastructure] construction work

Queries:
1. What is the total sanctioned amount for [name of road/project]?
2. What is the name of the contractor to whom the work has been awarded?
3. What is the prescribed completion date of the work?
4. How much amount has been paid to the contractor till date?
5. What is the quality inspection report and by whom was it certified?
6. Please provide the measurement book/work completion certificate for the said work.

---

=== RTI TEMPLATE FOR GOVERNMENT JOB RECRUITMENT ===

Subject: Seeking information regarding [post name] recruitment process

Queries:
1. Please provide my marks/scores in each paper/stage of examination for post [name] in [recruitment year].
2. Please provide the qualifying marks for each stage of selection.
3. Please provide the total number of vacancies in each category (UR/OBC/SC/ST/EWS).
4. Please provide the final merit list with roll numbers.
5. What is the cut-off score for [my category] for the post?
6. Please provide the answer key used for evaluation.

=== RTI TEMPLATE FOR LAND RECORDS ===

Subject: Seeking land record information for [survey number/plot number]

Queries:
1. Please provide the current ownership records (Khatauni/Patta) for Survey No. [X], Village [Y], Tehsil [Z].
2. Please provide the mutation records (Dakhil Kharij) for the said land for last 10 years.
3. Is there any encumbrance, government acquisition notice, or restriction on the said land?
4. Please provide the current revenue map showing the boundaries of the said land.

=== LABOUR RIGHTS REFERENCE ===

MINIMUM WAGES ACT 1948:
Every employer must pay minimum wages as notified by Central/State government. Check your state Labour Department website for current minimum wages by industry category. File complaint with Labour Commissioner or Industrial Tribunal for violation.

PAYMENT OF WAGES ACT 1936:
Wages must be paid by: 7th of next month (if workforce <1000 employees), 10th of next month (if >1000 employees). Unauthorized deductions not permitted. File complaint with Payment of Wages Authority (Labour Commissioner).

PROVIDENT FUND (EPF):
- Applicable to establishments with 20+ employees
- Employees earning up to Rs. 15,000 basic+DA must contribute
- Employee contribution: 12% of basic salary; Employer contribution: 12%
- File grievance at: epfigms.gov.in or call 1800-118-005

GRATUITY (Payment of Gratuity Act):
- Entitled after 5 years of continuous service
- Formula: Last drawn salary × 15/26 × years of service
- File complaint with Labour Commissioner if not paid

ESI (Employees' State Insurance):
- For establishments employing 10+ persons
- Employees earning up to Rs. 21,000 get medical benefits
- ESI hospitals provide free treatment to insured workers

MATERNITY BENEFIT ACT 2017:
- 26 weeks paid maternity leave for first 2 children
- 12 weeks for 3rd child onwards
- Cannot be dismissed during maternity leave
- Work from home option after maternity leave
- Crèche facility in establishments with 50+ employees

SEXUAL HARASSMENT AT WORKPLACE (POSH ACT 2013):
- Every employer with 10+ employees must have Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)
- Sexual harassment includes: unwelcome physical/verbal/non-verbal conduct of sexual nature
- Complaint must be made within 3 months
- ICC must complete inquiry within 60 days
- For organizations with <10 employees, complaint to Local Complaints Committee (under District Collector)
- Helpline: SHe-Box portal at shebox.nic.in
