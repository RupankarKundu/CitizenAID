MAINTENANCE AND WELFARE OF PARENTS ACT 2007

RIGHT TO MAINTENANCE FROM CHILDREN
Senior citizens (60+ years) and parents have the legal right to claim maintenance from children. This includes:
- Monthly maintenance for food, clothing, shelter, medical treatment
- Maintenance from son, daughter, and even grandchildren if children are not alive
- Children's spouses (daughter-in-law, son-in-law) also liable if they inherited property from the senior citizen
- Maximum maintenance: up to Rs. 10,000/month (state governments can increase this)
- Maintenance Tribunal can order monthly payment within 90 days

HOW TO CLAIM MAINTENANCE
Step 1: File application before Maintenance Tribunal (Sub-Divisional Magistrate in most states)
Step 2: No lawyer needed — can apply in person
Step 3: State the monthly expenses and income of children
Step 4: Tribunal issues notice to children
Step 5: Order must be passed within 90 days
Step 6: If children don't pay: imprisonment up to 1 month

PROTECTION FROM EVICTION FROM OWN PROPERTY
If senior citizen has transferred property to children and is being mistreated:
- The transfer can be CANCELLED/REVOKED if children fail to maintain them
- Apply to Maintenance Tribunal for cancellation of property transfer
- This is a powerful provision — children who took property must maintain parents

RIGHT TO LIVE IN HOME WITHOUT EVICTION
Senior citizen cannot be evicted from their own home by children or relatives. File complaint with Maintenance Tribunal for protection order.

ABUSE AND NEGLECT OF SENIOR CITIZENS
Physical, mental, emotional, financial abuse of senior citizens is punishable. File complaint with:
- Local police station
- Senior Citizen Helpline: 14567 (National, 24 hours free)
- District Maintenance Tribunal
- State Social Welfare Department

SENIOR CITIZEN HELPLINES AND SUPPORT
- National Senior Citizen Helpline: 14567
- Elder Line: 14567 (run by Ministry of Social Justice)
- Agewell Foundation: 1800-180-1253 (toll-free)
- HelpAge India: 1800-180-1253

GOVERNMENT SCHEMES FOR SENIOR CITIZENS

PENSION SCHEMES
Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS):
- For BPL families, age 60+ years
- Rs. 200/month for age 60-79, Rs. 500/month for age 80+
- Apply at local Gram Panchayat or Municipal Corporation
- Documents: age proof, BPL card, bank account

Atal Pension Yojana (APY):
- For organised and unorganised sector workers
- Guaranteed pension of Rs. 1,000 to Rs. 5,000 per month after age 60
- Join through any bank

National Pension System (NPS):
- Voluntary pension scheme for all citizens
- Tax benefits available
- Open NPS account at any bank or online at enps.nsdl.com

PRADHAN MANTRI VAYA VANDANA YOJANA (PMVVY)
- For senior citizens 60+ years
- Guaranteed 7.4% annual return
- Available at LIC
- Investment up to Rs. 15 lakhs
- Monthly pension guaranteed for 10 years

HEALTH SCHEMES FOR SENIOR CITIZENS
Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY:
- Free health insurance up to Rs. 5 lakhs per year for poor families
- Covers hospitalization, surgery, pre/post hospitalization
- Check eligibility at pmjay.gov.in or call 14555
- No age limit — senior citizens covered

Senior Citizen Health Insurance:
- Mandatory for insurance companies to offer health insurance to senior citizens
- Cannot be denied insurance only on basis of age (IRDAI guidelines)
- Pre-existing diseases must be covered after waiting period

RASTRIYA VAYOSHRI YOJANA
- Free assistive devices (hearing aids, wheelchairs, spectacles, artificial limbs) for BPL senior citizens
- Apply at Social Welfare Department
- Camps organized regularly for distribution

CONCESSIONS AND BENEFITS FOR SENIOR CITIZENS
Railways:
- 40% concession for men above 60 years
- 50% concession for women above 58 years
- Book at railways counter or online — must carry age proof

Air Travel: some airlines offer senior citizen discounts — check airline website

Income Tax Benefits:
- Tax exemption limit: Rs. 3 lakhs for senior citizens (60-80 years), Rs. 5 lakhs for super senior citizens (80+)
- Medical insurance premium deduction: up to Rs. 50,000 per year
- Medical expenditure deduction: Rs. 1 lakh for specified diseases

Banks:
- Higher interest rates on fixed deposits (typically 0.25-0.50% extra)
- Priority banking services
- Senior citizen savings scheme at post office: 8.2% interest per year

SENIOR CITIZEN SAFETY
If a senior citizen is being cheated, harassed, or is in danger:
- Call 14567 (Senior Citizen Helpline)
- Call 100 (Police)
- Many police stations have Senior Citizen cells
- Register with local police station's senior citizen register for regular check-ins
- Vaya Mitra scheme: volunteers visit isolated senior citizens

PROPERTY RIGHTS OF SENIOR CITIZENS
- Senior citizen's property cannot be transferred without their free consent
- Any transfer under coercion/undue influence is voidable
- Tribunal can cancel such transfers
- Senior citizens can write a Will anytime — it can be changed
- Power of Attorney: be careful about giving General Power of Attorney to anyone
